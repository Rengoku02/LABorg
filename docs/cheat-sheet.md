# LabStock cheat sheet

LabStock shows what the lab already has before you buy, and keeps track of what's in the
freezers. Keep ordering in Quartzy as usual: LabStock only reads it.

## Before you order: search

- Open LabStock and type a **name**, a **catalog #** (any formatting: `11965-092`, `11965092`,
  `CST 2118S`) or **paste the vendor's product page** into the search box.
- Results: **In the lab** (shared first), **Probably: ask X** (not checked yet), **On order**
  (someone is buying it now), **Bought before** (the last 24 months).
- Faster:
  - **Address bar:** add LabStock as a search shortcut with the keyword `ls`, then type
    `ls 2118S`. Instructions on the *Search from anywhere* page (`/s/setup/`).
  - **Bookmarklet:** drag *Check LabStock* from that page to your bookmarks bar. On any
    vendor page, click it (or select a catalog # first).

## Sharing levels (your choice, per item)

| Level | What others see |
|---|---|
| Shared | Where it is: take some, it's lab stock |
| Ask first (default) | Where it is, and that they should ask you first |
| Restricted | Only that you have it and to ask you, never where it is |

Change it on the item's page (*Who may see where it is?*). Material under an MTA is always
restricted.

## Getting your stock in, without typing at the bench

- **Claim your history:** every Monday you may get an email listing things you bought. One tap
  each: *Still have it* (where?), *Used up*, *Gave it to someone*, *In the shared stock*,
  *Not mine*, *Did this arrive?*, or *Later*. The link in the email works for 7 days and opens
  only your list.
- **Add what I have:** for things from a previous lab, a collaborator, a gift, made in the lab,
  or bought outside Quartzy.
- **Register a box:** name, owner, where it is, and a photo of the lid. People can find the box
  before anything in it is listed.

## Duplicate alerts

If you request something the lab already has, LabStock may email you within minutes: *"Before
you order: Priya has CST 2118S in -80 A"*. It asks **"Have you placed the vendor order yet?"**

- Not yet: *Borrow from Priya* (she gets an email; reply to arrange it), *Share Raj's order*,
  or *Cancel my Quartzy request*.
- Yes: *I'll cancel with the vendor*, or *Keep it, offer the spare*.
- Either way: *Not the same thing* (it won't flag that pair again for you), or *I need my own*
  (say why).

Every link shows a confirm page first; nothing happens until you press *Confirm*. Later
follow-ups (the vendor confirmed, you borrowed it) are on *My duplicate alerts* (`/guard/mine/`).

Only you receive your alerts. The PI sees lab totals only.

## Asking

- **Ask a holder** from an item's page or a search result: they get an email, and the reply
  comes straight to you.
- **Ask the lab** (`/asks/`): one lab-wide ask per person per day. People get at most one ask
  email a day, sent in batches (late morning and mid-afternoon), unless you mark it urgent.

## Privacy

- The PI sees **lab totals and who holds what**, never what you searched for, how you answered
  an alert, or why you ordered anyway. No per-person duplicate counts, no leaderboards.
- Searches are kept 30 days without their text, only to count "searched before buying" for the
  whole lab.
- You're thanked by name for lending only if you opt in.

Something awkward? Use *Something awkward? Tell us* at the bottom of any page.
